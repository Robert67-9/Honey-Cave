8. Seller onboarding and duties

8.1 A seller may be required to provide Ghana Card verification, live selfie, telephone and email, address and GPS location, payout details matching the verified seller, business documents, authorised representatives, shop or inventory evidence and declared supplier details.

8.3 The seller must keep registration, inventory, pricing, payout and fulfilment information current; cooperate with inspections, test purchases and investigations; and maintain any licences, approvals and tax records required for the seller's activity.

9.1 A seller may list only a product that it possesses, directly controls or can reliably obtain through a declared and approved supplier within the stated preparation time.

9.3 The seller remains fully responsible for the supplier's conduct and for product authenticity, quality, conformity, availability, packaging, warranty, returns and refunds. Supplier approval does not transfer responsibility to HoneyCave Market.

10. Prohibited seller and middleman conduct

  • Redirecting a rider to an undeclared supplier or pickup point.
  • Using copied or misleading images to misrepresent stock.
  • Substituting a product without informed buyer approval recorded through the platform.
  • Asking a buyer to pay the seller, rider or supplier outside the approved checkout process.
  • Using another person's identity or payout account, or opening duplicate accounts to evade restrictions.
  • Attempting to cash out funds linked to an incomplete, reversed, returned or disputed order.

11. Product listings, pricing and stock

11.1 A listing must accurately state the product, brand, model, condition, quantity, total price, key specifications, material limitations, warranty, estimated preparation time and applicable return restrictions.

11.2 Orders remain subject to availability and verification. Where payment has been captured and the order is cancelled without buyer fault, the applicable refund process shall begin.

11.3 A seller must confirm stock before rider dispatch. Repeated stock failure may result in a failed-pickup fee, reduced listing visibility, longer settlement or suspension.

12. Orders, payments, balances and settlement

12.1 Payment must be successfully authorised or received before processing unless HoneyCave Market expressly offers another method. Payment services may be provided by an appropriately authorised third party whose terms also apply.

12.2 Buyer funds shall not become immediately withdrawable by a seller. Availability for cashout depends on successful pickup and delivery, buyer acceptance or other valid proof, expiry of the applicable settlement period, fraud checks and absence of a live dispute or reversal.

12.3 Recommended settlement periods are 48 to 72 hours for new sellers, 24 hours or the next business day for established sellers, and longer for high-value, high-risk, returned or disputed orders. HoneyCave Market may publish or adjust risk-based periods reasonably and prospectively.

12.4 A platform balance is an accounting record, not a bank deposit, and remains subject to provider settlement, refund, chargeback, reserve, deduction and verification rules. HoneyCave Market shall not describe a payment arrangement as escrow unless it has been lawfully structured and approved for that purpose.

14.1 A rider may be required to provide identity, contact and address information, emergency contact, licence where applicable, vehicle registration, insurance or other legally required documents, vehicle type, parcel capacity, payout details and selected delivery zones.

14.3 The rider is responsible for roadworthiness, lawful operation, personal safety equipment, traffic compliance and accurate status information. HoneyCave Market may restrict an expired, unsafe, false or incomplete rider account.

15. Delivery zones and assignment rules

15.1 Each rider may select a primary zone, neighbouring backup zones, working hours, operating radius, vehicle type and parcel capacity. Assignments should consider pickup and delivery coordinates, availability, capacity, distance, workload and performance.

15.2 Before acceptance, the rider should be shown the general route, estimated distance, parcel category, fee and special handling information. A rider may decline an out-of-zone, unsafe, oversized, prohibited or unsuitable assignment without improper penalty.

15.3 Same-zone orders should ordinarily use one local rider. Cross-zone orders may use neighbouring riders or a documented transfer hub. Intercity and bulky orders may use approved couriers or suitable vehicles. Every custody transfer must be recorded.

16. Pickup, packaging and parcel custody

16.1 Before dispatch, the seller shall confirm stock, product identity, quantity, pickup location, packaging and relevant serial numbers. HoneyCave Market may request a current photograph or video.

16.2 At pickup, the rider shall attend the verified location, compare reasonable visible details, complete the pickup OTP or QR process, record required identifiers and document the sealed parcel's visible condition. The rider does not certify technical quality or authenticity.

16.3 If redirected to an undeclared supplier or location, the rider must pause and report the incident. HoneyCave Market may verify, cancel, reassign, charge the seller, compensate the rider or restrict the account.

16.4 The seller must use safe packaging appropriate to the item. The rider must exercise reasonable care while the parcel is in the rider's custody and must not open a sealed parcel without authorisation.

17. Delivery, buyer acceptance and proof

17.1 At delivery, the rider shall confirm the recipient or order details, permit reasonable visible inspection where applicable, obtain the OTP only after receipt and acceptance, and record the required time, location, signature, photograph or serial-number evidence.

17.2 The buyer should confirm general conformity, quantity, obvious damage, substitution and tampering. Acceptance does not waive rights relating to hidden defects, warranty or other non-excludable remedies.

17.3 A user must not pressure, deceive or collude with another person concerning an OTP, delivery record, location, review, refund or proof of delivery.
