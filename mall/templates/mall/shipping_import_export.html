Questions

Frequently Asked

How do I book a package pickup?

Use the "Ship a Package" form above. Enter pickup and destination addresses, package weight, and a pickup date — a driver will arrive during your chosen window.

What tracking information will I receive?

Once booked, you'll get a unique tracking code (e.g. HC-8849201). Enter it in the Track Shipment box above anytime.

Are packages insured against damage or loss?

You can add full-value declared insurance (+GH₵ 125, up to GH₵ 37,500 coverage) during booking for extra peace of mind.

Does Honey Cave ship internationally?

Yes — we ship worldwide, with customs processing handled as part of the booking.
